Privacy Policy - Mon Auto Poster

Last updated: 04/10/2026

We only access the permissions the owner grants (uploading videos). We do not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties. Access tokens are stored only in the owner's private automation workspace and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app settings.
